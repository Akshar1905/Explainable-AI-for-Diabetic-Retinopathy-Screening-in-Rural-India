Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

ADR 0001: Equal-Coverage Safety Veto

A configuration is inadmissible if it sends more referable patients home than the standalone classifier at the same coverage. Miss count is non-negotiable.

ADR 0002: Keep the Grad-CAM Spatial Gate

The spatial agreement check between Grad-CAM regional attention and U-Net lesion coordinates must remain active to prevent spurious feature hallucination.

Safety Invariants & Clinical Guardrails
  • Always Escalate Level 4: Proliferative Diabetic Retinopathy (PDR) is never auto-cleared or auto-referred without clinician confirmation.
  • Temperature Scaling: Raw softmax probabilities are never treated as calibrated risk; temperature $T = 2.1414$ is applied.
  • Single Preprocessing Seam: Training and inference share exact byte-identical preprocessing logic (448×448, Green CLAHE).
  • Sealed External Test Set: Messidor-2 dataset is protected under recorded cryptographical seal.
